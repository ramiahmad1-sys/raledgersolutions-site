RA Ledger Solutions, a DBA of Alquds Accounting & Tax Services LLC, Staten Island, NY. Website: raledgersolutions.com

What these messages are

Our automated phone assistant answers inbound calls to the business. When a caller leaves a callback request, one internal text alert is sent to the business owner's own mobile number with the caller's name, callback number and reason for calling. Customers, callers and members of the public never receive text messages from this program. There is no marketing content.

How the business owner opts in

This program has exactly one recipient: the business owner. The owner sets their own mobile number as the alert destination in the phone assistant's configuration, then opts in by texting the keyword START (or YES) from that phone to the RA Ledger Solutions business number, (718) 285-6959. Alerts are sent only after this keyword opt-in. By texting START, the owner agrees to the following:

By texting START to (718) 285-6959, I agree to receive automated text alerts from RA Ledger Solutions about new call messages at this number. Msg frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Sample message

RA Ledger Solutions: New call message. Caller: [name], callback [phone number]. Reason: needs bookkeeping help.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP (also CANCEL, QUIT, END, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL, OPTOUT, REVOKE) at any time to stop alerts. Reply START to resubscribe. Reply HELP or INFO for help, or contact info@raledgersolutions.com or 718-285-6959.
