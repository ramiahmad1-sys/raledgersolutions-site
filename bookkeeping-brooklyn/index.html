Who we work with in Brooklyn

We work with Brooklyn owners in service businesses, retail shops, professional practices, nonprofits, property management and real estate. The work is done remotely through QuickBooks Online, so you do not need to travel to an office, and we are happy to meet by phone or video.

Why Brooklyn owners choose monthly bookkeeping

Busy Brooklyn owners often juggle several bank accounts, cards and payment apps, and the books slip until tax season. A monthly close keeps every account reconciled, so you know what you earned last month, which bills are coming and whether cash will cover payroll and rent.

Clean monthly books also make loan and lease applications, sales tax filings and year-end tax preparation faster, because the numbers are already in order when someone asks for them.

Common questions from Brooklyn owners

Do you only serve Staten Island?

No. Our office is in Staten Island, next door to Brooklyn, but most of the work is done remotely, so we serve small businesses in every Brooklyn neighborhood and across New York City.

Do I need to bring paperwork to an office?

No. Most bookkeeping is handled remotely through QuickBooks Online and secure bank feeds, so there is no need to drop off boxes of receipts.

My books are months behind. Can you catch them up?

Yes. Catch-up and cleanup work is common. We review where your records stand, quote the cleanup in writing, and then move you onto a monthly schedule. See QuickBooks cleanup for details.

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Monthly bookkeeping starts at $250 a month. Your fee depends on how many accounts and transactions you have. Cleanup and catch-up work is quoted after a free 20-minute consultation, and every fee is confirmed in writing before work begins.

Are you a CPA firm?

No. RA Ledger Solutions is not a CPA firm and does not provide audit, attest, review or compilation services. We keep your books current and accurate so your tax preparer has clean records to work from.

Can we meet in person?

Most Brooklyn clients work with us entirely by phone, video and QuickBooks Online. If an in-person meeting would help, ask during your consultation.
