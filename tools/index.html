        Questions owners ask

        Is anything I type saved or sent?

        No. Both tools run entirely in your web browser. Your answers and numbers never leave your device, and they're gone as soon as you close or refresh the page. Nothing is stored, and this page sets no cookies.

        Who are these tools for?

        Small business owners in Staten Island, Brooklyn and across New York City who want a quick, honest look at their books and cash. You don't need QuickBooks to use them, although a few tips mention it.

        How accurate are the results?

        They're a friendly starting point, not a verdict. Results are based only on what you enter, and real businesses have seasons, taxes and one-time costs a quick check can't see. For decisions, talk with a qualified professional.

        What happens on the free 20-minute call?

        You talk with the accountant who would handle your books (CMA, EA, CTP, CSCA, MBA) about where things stand and what would help most. There's no pressure and no obligation. The call is informational and doesn't create a client relationship. Any work begins only after we agree on scope and fees in writing.
