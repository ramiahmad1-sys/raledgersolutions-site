Our services

Monthly Bookkeeping

The core of what we do. Each month we categorize every transaction, reconcile your bank and credit card accounts, and deliver a profit and loss report and balance sheet you can actually read. No more guessing whether you made money last month.

QuickBooks Online Setup & Cleanup

QuickBooks is only as good as the way it’s set up. We build company files with a chart of accounts that fits your business, connect your bank feeds, and train you on what to do — and what not to do — day to day.

Already have QuickBooks and it’s a mess? Cleanup is a common request: years of miscoded transactions, duplicate entries, unreconciled accounts, balances that never quite made sense. We’ll scope it and tell you what it will take before you commit to anything.

Cash Flow & Financial Consulting

Cash flow projections, budgeting, or a second opinion before a big decision. Book time and get a straight answer instead of a sales pitch. Where a question calls for legal or investment advice, we’ll say so and point you to the right professional.
