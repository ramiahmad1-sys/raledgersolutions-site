About RA Ledger Solutions

RA Ledger Solutions is a Staten Island bookkeeping and accounting practice. Every client works directly with the same accountant, from the first consultation through each monthly close. There is no hand-off to a junior bookkeeper or a call center.

QuickBooks ProAdvisor · CMA · EA · CTP · CSCA · MBA

Who does the work

Your accountant has about 25 years of accounting experience, a B.S. in business management and finance, cum laude, from Brooklyn College, and an MBA from the New York Institute of Technology.

  • Advanced QuickBooks Online ProAdvisor, certified at QuickBooks Online Level 2
  • CMA, Certified Management Accountant
  • EA, Enrolled Agent, enrolled to practice before the Internal Revenue Service
  • CTP, Certified Treasury Professional
  • CSCA, Certified in Strategy and Competitive Analysis
  • MBA, Master of Business Administration

How we work

  • One accountant on your books every month, who already knows your business
  • Scope and fees in a written engagement letter before any work begins
  • Plain-language monthly reports, and straight answers when you have a question
  • Your bank accounts and your QuickBooks file always stay in your name

Where we work

Our office is in Staten Island, and most of the work is done remotely through QuickBooks Online, so we serve small businesses in Staten Island, Brooklyn and the rest of New York City. Consultations are by appointment.

Important to know

RA Ledger Solutions is not a CPA firm and does not provide audit, attest, review or compilation services. We do not take custody or control of client funds; your bank accounts stay in your name.
