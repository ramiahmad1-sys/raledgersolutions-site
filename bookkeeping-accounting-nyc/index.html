Why owners work with us remotely

Because the books live in QuickBooks Online, there is no need to drop off receipts or travel to an office. You keep full ownership of your bank accounts and your QuickBooks file, and you get the same accountant every month who already knows your business.

Common questions

Do you work with businesses outside Staten Island and Brooklyn?

Yes. We work with small businesses in all five boroughs. Most of the work is done remotely through QuickBooks Online, so where your business is located does not change the service.

How does remote bookkeeping work?

You give us invited access to QuickBooks Online and connect your bank and card feeds. Each month we categorize and reconcile everything, send your reports, and answer questions by phone, video or email.

How much does bookkeeping cost?

Monthly bookkeeping starts at $250 a month. Your fee depends on how many accounts and transactions you have. Cleanup and catch-up work is quoted after a free 20-minute consultation, and every fee is confirmed in writing before work begins.

Do you prepare tax returns or audits?

RA Ledger Solutions is not a CPA firm and does not provide audit, attest, review or compilation services. We keep your books current and accurate so your tax preparer has clean, reconciled records to work from.

My books are behind. Can you catch them up?

Yes. We review where your records stand, quote the catch-up in writing, and then move you onto a monthly schedule. See QuickBooks cleanup for details.
